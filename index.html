ABOUT THE APP

A focused workflow for ThinkDifferently.

ThinkDifferently Shorts Studio is a private tool used by the channel owner to prepare educational Shorts, upload drafts privately, and manage selected videos after explicit approval. It uses YouTube API Services to verify the authorized channel, inspect video information, upload videos and perform approved management actions.

일반 사용자를 위한 가입 서비스가 아닙니다. 이 사이트는 앱 소개와 개인정보 안내용이며 Google 인증 정보를 입력받지 않습니다.
